STORY
-----
The Mushroom Kingdom is in trouble once again! The evil Bowser has kidnapped the seven kings of the Mushroom World and turned them into strange creatures. Mario and Luigi must travel through eight different worlds to defeat Bowser's minions, rescue the kings, and save the princess.

GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Insert the Super Mario Bros. 3 Game Pak into your Nintendo Entertainment System.
2. Press the Power Button to turn on the system.
3. Press the Start Button to begin the game.
4. Choose 1 Player or 2 Players game.
5. Press the Start Button again to start playing.

CONTROLLER
----------
+ Control Pad: Move Mario/Luigi left, right, up (to enter doors, climb vines, etc.), down (to crouch, go down pipes).
A Button: Jump, pick up items, confirm selections.
B Button: Run, fire attacks (when powered up), cancel selections.
Start Button: Pause game, start game.
Select Button: Not used in this game.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
The objective of each level is to reach the end point (usually marked by a flagpole) while avoiding enemies and obstacles. Collect coins for extra lives (100 coins = 1 life). Collect power-ups to gain special abilities.

ITEMS
-----
Super Mushroom: Makes Mario/Luigi grow to Super size, able to break bricks and take one hit without dying.
Fire Flower: Allows Mario/Luigi to throw fireballs when in Super form.
Super Leaf: Transforms Mario/Luigi into Raccoon form, allowing flight and tail attacks.
Tanooki Suit: Similar to Super Leaf but also allows statue transformation for temporary invincibility.
Hammer Suit: Allows Mario/Luigi to throw hammers and resist fire attacks.
Starman: Makes Mario/Luigi temporarily invincible and able to defeat enemies by touch.
1-Up Mushroom: Grants an extra life.
Coin: Collect 100 for an extra life.

ENEMIES
-------
Goomba: Walks slowly; defeated by jumping on it or hitting with fireball/hammer/etc.
Koopa Troopa: Walks in its shell; can be jumped on to make it retreat into shell, then kicked.
Paratroopa: Flying Koopa Troopa; loses wings when jumped on.
Buzzy Beetle: Black beetle resistant to fireballs; can be jumped on and kicked.
Spiny: Red creature with spikes; cannot be jumped on; must be hit with fireball/hammer/etc. or knocked loose from ceiling.
Fly Guy: Flies and drops items; can be jumped on or hit with projectiles.
Piranha Plant: Emerges from pipes; can be defeated with fireball/hammer/etc. when out of pipe, or avoided.

WORLDS
------
World 1: Grass Land
World 2: Desert Hill
World 3: Water Land
World 4: Giant Hill
World 5: Ice Land
World 6: Sky Land
World 7: Pipe Land
World 8: Dark Land

Each world has multiple levels, including regular levels, fortress levels, and a final castle level with a boss fight.

TIPS
----
- Save special items (like Super Leaves) for difficult sections.
- In World 2 (Desert Hill), remember that you can't jump on Spinies - use fireballs or hammers.
- In World 3 (Water Land), you can swim by pressing the A button repeatedly.
- Look for hidden blocks that often contain power-ups or 1-Up mushrooms.
- In fortress levels, defeat Boom Boom by jumping on his head three times.
- Use the warp whistles in Worlds 1, 2, and 3 to skip to later worlds.
