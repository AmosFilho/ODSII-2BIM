# Super Mario Bros. 3 Overview

Super Mario Bros. 3 is a platform game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES). Released in 1988 in Japan and 1990 in North America, it is the third installment in the Super Mario series.

## Story
The Mushroom Kingdom is in trouble once again! The evil Bowser has kidnapped the seven kings of the Mushroom World and turned them into strange creatures. Mario and Luigi must travel through eight different worlds to defeat Bowser's minions, rescue the kings, and save the princess.

## Gameplay
The objective of each level is to reach the end point (usually marked by a flagpole) while avoiding enemies and obstacles. Players collect coins for extra lives (100 coins = 1 life) and collect power-ups to gain special abilities.

## Worlds
The game features eight distinct worlds:
- World 1: Grass Land
- World 2: Desert Hill
- World 3: Water Land
- World 4: Giant Hill
- World 5: Ice Land
- World 6: Sky Land
- World 7: Pipe Land
- World 8: Dark Land

Each world contains multiple levels, including regular levels, fortress levels, and a final castle level with a boss fight.

## Controls
- **Control Pad**: Move Mario/Luigi left, right, up (to enter doors, climb vines, etc.), down (to crouch, go down pipes)
- **A Button**: Jump, pick up items, confirm selections
- **B Button**: Run, fire attacks (when powered up), cancel selections
- **Start Button**: Pause game, start game
- **Select Button**: Not used in this game
