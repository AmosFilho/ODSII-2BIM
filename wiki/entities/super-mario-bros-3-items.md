# Super Mario Bros. 3 Items

This page describes all the collectible items and power-ups available in Super Mario Bros. 3.

## Power-Ups

### Super Mushroom
- Makes Mario/Luigi grow to Super size
- Able to break bricks
- Can take one hit without dying (returns to Small Mario)

### Fire Flower
- Allows Mario/Luigi to throw fireballs when in Super form
- Fireballs can defeat many enemies from a distance

### Super Leaf
- Transforms Mario/Luigi into Raccoon form
- Allows flight (by building speed and pressing A)
- Enables tail attacks (spin attack with B button)

### Tanooki Suit
- Similar to Super Leaf but also allows statue transformation
- Press down+B to turn into a statue for temporary invincibility
- Statue form can also be used to defeat certain enemies from below

### Hammer Suit
- Allows Mario/Luigi to throw hammers (press B)
- Hammers can defeat many enemies and break blocks
- Provides resistance to fire attacks

### Starman
- Makes Mario/Luigi temporarily invincible
- Able to defeat enemies by touch
- Music changes while active
- Effect lasts for a limited time

## Other Items

### 1-Up Mushroom
- Grants an extra life
- Often found in hidden blocks or as rewards

### Coin
- Collect 100 coins for an extra life
- Found throughout levels, often in rows or clusters

## Usage Tips
- Save special items (like Super Leaves) for difficult sections
- Fire flowers are particularly useful in worlds with many ground enemies
- Raccoon flight (Super Leaf) helps reach hidden areas and avoid difficult sections
- Hammer suits excel against fire-based enemies and in fortress levels
