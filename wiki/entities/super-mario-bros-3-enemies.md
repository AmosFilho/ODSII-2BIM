# Super Mario Bros. 3 Enemies

This page lists the enemies encountered in Super Mario Bros. 3 and how to defeat them.

## Common Enemies

### Goomba
- Walks slowly
- Defeated by jumping on it or hitting with fireball/hammer/etc.

### Koopa Troopa
- Walks in its shell
- Can be jumped on to make it retreat into shell, then kicked

### Paratroopa
- Flying Koopa Troopa
- Loses wings when jumped on

### Buzzy Beetle
- Black beetle resistant to fireballs
- Can be jumped on and kicked

### Spiny
- Red creature with spikes
- Cannot be jumped on
- Must be hit with fireball/hammer/etc. or knocked loose from ceiling

### Fly Guy
- Flies and drops items
- Can be jumped on or hit with projectiles

### Piranha Plant
- Emerges from pipes
- Can be defeated with fireball/hammer/etc. when out of pipe, or avoided

## Boss Enemies
Each world ends with a fortress or castle boss (e.g., Boom Boom in fortresses, Bowser's children in castles).

## Tips for Specific Enemies
- In World 2 (Desert Hill), remember that you can't jump on Spinies - use fireballs or hammers.
